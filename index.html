How do I purchase agricultural land near Nagpur legally? +

Under Maharashtra land laws, agricultural land buyers need valid farmer status certification, a current 7/12 extract (Satbara Utara), 8A extract, a 30-year title search report, and boundary demarcation. KIMI Properties coordinates verification across Parseoni, Umred, and Dunda Khairi. See our Property Buying Checklist.
