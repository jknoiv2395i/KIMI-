Financing & Bank Approvals

Home Loan & Commercial Property Finance Guide for Nagpur Buyers

Banks in Nagpur run separate legal and technical valuations before releasing a sanction letter. Having the right file ready cuts processing time down from four weeks to ten days.

1. Property Papers Banks Require in Nagpur

Whether applying with SBI, HDFC, ICICI, or local cooperative banks, technical clearance depends on municipal or MIDC status:

  • Registered chain of previous Sale Deeds (last 13 to 30 years) and Nil Encumbrance Certificate.
  • For plots: Clear NA order and NIT, NMRDA, or NMC sanctioned layout plan with R.L. (Release Letter).
  • For flats: Sanctioned building plan, Commencement/Occupancy Certificate (or completion papers for older resale blocks), and MahaRERA registration number for under-construction projects.
  • Updated Property Tax receipt and Society NOC for resale apartments.

2. Income & Financial File for Salaried and Business Buyers

Industrial unit owners and self-employed professionals buying shops or sheds usually need 2 to 3 years of audited financials:

  • Last 3 years ITR with computation of income and profit/loss statements (for business applicants).
  • 6 months primary current and savings bank statements plus existing EMI repayment schedule.
  • Keep 15% to 25% down payment plus Maharashtra stamp duty and registration fees liquid outside loan proceeds.

Common Questions

Do banks approve loans on resale flats in Manish Nagar and Wanadongri? +

Yes, provided the building holds proper sanctioned plans, clear sale deed chain, and updated municipal tax / property card records.

Are industrial plots in Butibori MIDC eligible for business loans? +

Yes. Banks and NBFCs finance MIDC plots and running factory sheds through MSME term loans or Lease Rental Discounting against verified lease deeds.
